### 🧑‍💻 About me

- 🤖 Since **2022**, I compete in the **French Robotics Cup (Eurobot)** with **[Team Opossum](https://github.com/Les-opossums)**.
- 🔧 On the team, I work across the whole stack:
  - **Mechanical design** with SolidWorks
  - **PCB design** with KiCad
  - **Low-level software** on the Zynq-7000 SoC
  - **Computer vision** on JeVois-Pro smart cameras

### 🚀 Featured projects

- 💾 **[Zynq_SW_Eurobot](https://github.com/Les-opossums/Zynq_SW_Eurobot)** — Low-level software for the Opossum robot's Zynq-7000 SoC.
- 🔌 **[Zynq_HW_Eurobot_2025](https://github.com/Les-opossums/Zynq_HW_Eurobot_2025)** — Hardware and PCB design for the 2025 robot.
